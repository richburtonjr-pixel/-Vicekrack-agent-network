# ViceKrack Content House: approved visual direction

Approved by the owner on 2026-10-13. This is the reference for the headquarters redesign, which
comes **after** the Research-to-Video workflow. Nothing here changes the video pipeline or the xAI
integration.

## The eight agents

Four men and four women. Ordinary people working at a modern content production company.

| Agent | Role | Real ViceKrack work today |
|---|---|---|
| Ricky | Leadership | Your approvals and reviews |
| Kiara | Research | Research (Scout, Grok research) |
| Jax | Trends | Coming soon (no audience or trend data yet) |
| Layla | Strategy | Story selection and angles |
| Ace | Risk Review | Verification, rumor labels, quality checks |
| Sienna | Content Creator | Scripts, clips, narration, captions, rendering |
| Kai | Analytics | Coming soon (no performance data yet) |
| Nia | Operations | Workflow progress and exports (no publishing) |

Appearance rules:

- The women include naturally dark-skinned Black women with realistic facial features and varied
  hair: locs, braids and natural textures.
- No exaggerated beauty-model looks, identical faces, overly glamorous clothing or futuristic
  costumes. Everyday workwear (the reference shows black VK hoodies and caps).
- Each agent has their own recognizable face, hairstyle and personality, consistent across every
  scene, department and camera angle.
- A reusable **character reference library** (front, three-quarter and profile per agent) keeps them
  consistent. It will live in `docs/images/characters/`.

Reference: [reference-v1.jpg](images/characters/reference-v1.jpg), used **only** for the characters,
not for the office size or layout.

## The building

[hq-architecture-reference.jpg](images/characters/hq-architecture-reference.jpg) is the building
reference:

- an enormous, bright white, glass-walled atrium with several visible floors and very high ceilings;
- floor-to-ceiling windows, natural sunlight, indoor trees, comfortable seating and city views;
- wide balconies and glass walkways;
- separate, spacious departments with room between workstations;
- a wide view that makes the building feel enormous, with people at realistic human scale.

## Activity and honesty

- Agents walk between departments, sit at computers, and hold occasional conversations and meetings
  related to their roles.
- **Task status comes only from the real ViceKrack system.** Decorative movement is labelled as
  atmosphere and is never presented as proof of work. A department with no real job yet shows
  "Coming soon", and agent counts are real counts.

## Build order (when the headquarters work starts)

1. A wide view of all eight agents inside the building.
2. Individual department views.
3. Smooth navigation between them.

Lightweight techniques only: realistic visuals without a full 3D game engine.
